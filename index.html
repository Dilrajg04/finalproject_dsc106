Lines show the 5-model average (10-year smoothing); shaded bands show the range across the 5 models.

Data & transformations. Near-surface air temperature (tas, monthly) from five CMIP6 climate models, read from the public CMIP6 archive on Google Cloud. For each city we take the model grid cell nearest to it (about 100–250 km wide, so coastal cities partly include ocean). Monthly values are averaged into yearly values, then each model is compared with its own 1850–1900 average, so differences between models' absolute temperatures cancel out. Historical runs cover 1850–2014; scenario runs cover 2015–2100. The map averages the 20 years ending in the selected year and the 5 models together.
